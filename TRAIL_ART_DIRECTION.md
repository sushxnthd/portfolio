# Trail artwork / Edition 01

Twelve newly authored original SVG plates. Each is a decorative concept illustration, not a screenshot, physical hardware image, actual clinical output or measured research chart. No external image, logo, raster texture or copied geometry is embedded.

## Art direction
- Swiss modular grids and strong hierarchy; industrial product schematics and instrument-like linework.
- 480×320 landscape and 360×480 portrait. Opaque, square-edged fields; high-contrast silhouettes; no rotation, no filters, no external font requests.
- Somno: sea-glass and charcoal; Hush: cobalt and cool paper; Kernellum: graphite, phosphor and pale technical paper; Green Groove: olive and lime; CopyCAT: parchment and lavender.
- Large primary marks, minimal text, deliberately varied compositions. Small labels are secondary at 90–180px trail widths.
- Concept diagrams are grounded in the portfolio records and explicitly identified as illustrations. Preserve the actual evidence and demo links separately.
