What exists

Somno combines four evidence streams: a short psychomotor-vigilance-style task, on-device facial and ocular features, Karolinska Sleepiness Scale input, and recent sleep history. Missing channels are renormalized rather than silently treated as zero evidence.

The production app also includes personal calibration, longitudinal recovery context, exact Android alarm scheduling, offline-first behavior, Supabase sync and user-facing data controls.

Technical core

The production Sleep Deprivation Index is centered on the user's own calibrated reference state. Reaction-time evidence carries base weight 0.40, facial / ocular evidence 0.25, subjective sleepiness 0.15 and recent sleep debt 0.20; unavailable evidence is renormalized.

SDI = clamp(50 + 10 × weighted personal-reference z-score, 0, 100)

The architecture favors inspectable signals and explicit quality handling over an opaque end-to-end score.

Design decisions

Personal normalization avoids treating a population score as an individual's baseline. Missing signals are handled by renormalizing the remaining evidence. Raw camera frames are processed on-device rather than retained for cloud sync.

The release is a concrete software artifact; these design decisions are not evidence of clinical efficacy.

What it does not claim

Somno does not claim to diagnose insomnia, certify driving safety, measure true real-time sleep stages, or establish clinical accuracy. Raw face images are not retained for cloud sync. The strongest next evidentiary step is prospective human validation of the complete combined system.
