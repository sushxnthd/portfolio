Can automated architecture search pick accelerator designs whose predicted advantages survive synthesis and place-and-route?

What I built

A parameterized INT8 tiled GEMM engine, analytical architecture model, RTL and simulation path, ECP5 synthesis/place-and-route loop, final-route-Fmax surrogate, active acquisition policy, and experiment ledger.

What survived

Nine initial routed architectures produced mean workload-ranking Spearman ρ = 0.944. A later bounded-feedback portfolio study completed 72 / 72 new final-route implementations and passed all 13 frozen confirmation criteria.

Boundary

This is final-route evidence, not physical-board latency, power, energy, or vendor-independent validation. K2 is board-ready; the next gate is real FPGA measurement.

THE FAILURE IS PART OF THE RESULT
v1.1 failed at 297 / 345.

All 48 misses were public order-6 loops. Instead of hiding the miss, I preserved the report, changed one scientific behavior, and reran the same corpus and gates. The resulting v1.2 reached 345 / 345. A separate hostile audit also invalidated broader novelty and query-efficiency claims before a narrower translation-base contribution survived as the stronger hypothesis.

What I built

A family-agnostic finite-operation discovery path, public-catalogue challenge, theorem-routed experiment loop, native external benchmarks, and reproducible GitHub Actions workflows.

What survived

The translation-base method has a conditional n(r+b) recovery bound, a matching Ω(n) transcript lower bound on a natural promise subclass, public cross-family correctness, and external Binary Operation Completion holdout evidence.

Boundary

The priority claim is deliberately narrow: plausible first-priority methodological contribution, not certified first-publication priority. The project also contains negative results where stronger specialist baselines win.

02 / CURRENT PURSUIT

Lucent: five seconds of signal.

The research question is intentionally aggressive: how much useful information about a person’s current functional state can be extracted from a five-second face video, without a wearable or a long test?

04 / FAILURE LEDGER

Claims I am not making.

A project becomes more credible when its boundaries are visible. These are the current limits I would expect a skeptical reviewer to care about.

KERNELLUM No board-level hardware result yet.

Place-and-route timing is not the same thing as measured latency, power, thermals, or energy on a physical device.

THEORICA No broad “AI discovers math” claim.

Several broad novelty claims were killed by the literature audit. The surviving translation-base claim is narrower and still needs independent priority/reproduction scrutiny.

LUCENT No five-second inference breakthrough yet.

The premise is a research target. The study has to determine what five seconds can and cannot support.

RESEARCH No publication-count theater.
